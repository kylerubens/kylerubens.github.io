My Design Journey

Active in the Design Community

Some of my greatest growth as a designer has come from learning alongside others. I’ve attended more than 150 design events across Dallas, contributed to M3+’s refreshed website and identify, participated in AIGA Dallas community design projects, and volunteered at Big Design and Circles Conferences.

Mentorship is Mutual

Leading others has been a valuable part of my design journey. I've shared what I’ve learned by hosting Figma workshops, judging design competitions, and participating on panels. As President of AIGA UT Dallas and Design Lead for UX Club at UT Dallas, I worked to introduce more students to the field of design.

My Philosophy

Insight-Driven Design

Meaningful design decisions don't happen alone. When creating BetterBalance, interviews, surveys, and iterative usability testing helped shape solutions that met real user needs.

Design That's Responsive and Accessible

Great experiences work for people with different needs and backgrounds. I helped Policy Angel build a responsive, scalable, and intuitive website and design system that meets accessibility and design conventions.

Inspiration Comes From Everywhere

Some of the best ideas come from unexpected places. Drawing from fitness apps, game interfaces, and language learning apps, I helped gamify students’ planning process to encourage proactivity.
